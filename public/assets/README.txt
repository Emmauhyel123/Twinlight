TWINLIGHT — ROMANTIC BRAND KIT

Created from the supplied Twinlight app files. Twinlight is a synchronous co-op web app for long-distance couples, with a shared lighthouse puzzle, Truth or Dare, Heart Sync, Love Notes, and a shared memory timeline.

Design direction: romantic, warm, intimate, elegant. Deep plum and wine backgrounds, blush rose, soft champagne gold, and warm ivory. The lighthouse motif is retained but integrated with a heart.
